> **Warning:** Heat stroke can kill. Confusion, fainting or a seizure in the heat = call **1669** now.

## Heat stroke (emergency)

**Signs:** very hot skin (red, dry or damp), body temperature **40 °C or higher**, confusion, slurred speech, fainting, seizure, fast strong pulse.

1. Call **1669**.
2. Move the person to shade or a cooler room.
3. **Cool them fast.** Best: put the body in cold water up to the neck. Otherwise soak with water and fan hard, put ice or cold packs on neck, armpits and groin.
4. Keep cooling until help arrives or they become clearly alert.
5. **Do not** give drinks if they are confused, vomiting or not fully awake.
6. If they stop breathing normally, start CPR.

## Heat exhaustion

**Signs:** heavy sweating, cool clammy skin, headache, dizziness, nausea, weakness, cramps, thirst, fast weak pulse.

1. Move to shade or a cool place. Stop all work.
2. Lie down, raise the legs a little.
3. Loosen or remove extra clothing.
4. Cool with wet cloths, a cool shower, fanning.
5. Give **sips** of water or ORS if they are awake and not vomiting.
6. **Get medical help** if they vomit, get worse, become confused, or are not better after **1 hour**.

## Home ORS recipe

Use this when you have no ORS packets. Packets ("เกลือแร่ ORS") from a pharmacy or convenience store are better.

1. **1 litre** of safe (boiled and cooled, or bottled) water.
2. **6 level teaspoons** of sugar.
3. **½ level teaspoon** of salt.
4. Stir until fully dissolved.
5. Taste it: it should be **no saltier than tears**. Too salty? Add more water.
6. Sip often. Throw away after **24 hours**.

> **Warning:** Measure carefully. Too much salt makes dehydration worse. Sports drinks are not a full substitute for ORS.

## Prevention

1. Drink before you feel thirsty. In heavy heat work, a cup (about 250 ml) every 15–20 minutes.
2. Avoid hard work from about 11:00 to 15:00. Rest in shade often.
3. Light, loose, light-coloured clothes and a hat.
4. Check on babies, older people and anyone sick. Never leave anyone in a parked car.

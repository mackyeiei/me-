1. **Shoes, headlamp and glasses next to your bed.** Night fires and earthquakes mean broken glass and dark stairs.
2. **Heavy stuff in the middle, close to your back.** Water and food against your spine, light things on the outside. The bag feels half the weight.
3. **Everything in zip bags.** Documents, phone, matches, meds. One flooded street soaks the whole bag.
4. **A bin bag is 5 tools:** rain poncho (cut a face hole), ground sheet, bag liner, water catcher, rubbish bag. Pack 2 heavy ones.
5. **Wrap duct tape around your water bottle or lighter.** You carry a few metres of tape for free.
6. **Meds in a 7-day pill box,** with a copy of your prescriptions.
7. **Sanitary pads are great wound dressings:** clean, sealed and very absorbent. Press hard on a bleeding wound.
8. **Cash in small notes, split up:** some in the bag, some in your wallet, some in a shoe.
9. **Check the bag twice a year.** Easy dates: **New Year and Songkran**. Swap food and water, charge the power bank, check that meds haven't expired.
10. **Pack for your real life:** pet food and a leash, baby supplies, a spare phone charger for parents.
11. **Practise once.** Walk your route to Rally 2 with the bag. You'll find out what's too heavy.

> **Warning:** A go-bag is only useful if you can grab it in 1 minute. Keep it by the door, not at the back of the wardrobe.

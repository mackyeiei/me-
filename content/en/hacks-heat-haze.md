## Heat

1. **Wet skin + moving air.** Wet towel on the neck, spray water on your arms, sit in front of a fan. Evaporation cools you far more than the fan alone.
2. **Above about 35 °C, a fan alone does not cool you.** You need water on the skin, a cool shower, or somewhere air-conditioned (a mall, a hospital lobby).
3. **Feet in a bucket of cool water.** Cheap, quiet and it works well for sleeping.
4. **Shade the room by day, ventilate at night.** Curtains closed on the sunny side, windows open after sunset.
5. **Check your pee.** Pale = OK. Dark = drink now. Add ORS if you are sweating a lot.
6. **A cold beer does not help.** Alcohol dries you out. So do energy drinks.
7. **Never leave a kid, an older person or a dog in a parked car**, even for 5 minutes.

## PM2.5 haze and smoke

1. **Check the AQI** (Air4Thai or IQAir apps) when you are online. Above 100 (US AQI), wear a mask outdoors. Above 150, limit time outside.
2. **N95 / KN95 only.** Cloth and surgical masks do little against PM2.5. Use both straps, pinch the nose clip, and breathe in hard: the mask should suck in a little.
3. **DIY air purifier.** Tape a HEPA or MERV-13 filter sheet to the **back (intake side)** of a fan and close the windows. A box fan with 4 filters taped into a cube (the "Corsi-Rosenthal box") is even better. Filter sheets cost a few hundred baht online.
4. **Make one clean room.** Pick the smallest bedroom, seal the gaps with tape, and run the filter fan there. Sleep there.
5. **Wet-mop, don't sweep.** Sweeping throws the dust back into the air.

> **Warning:** Confusion, fainting, or hot skin with no sweat = heat stroke. Call **1669** and start cooling now. See *Heat exhaustion, heat stroke and ORS*.

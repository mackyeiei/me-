1. **Set up your Medical ID now.** Responders can read it without unlocking your phone.
   iPhone: Health app → profile → Medical ID → turn on *Show When Locked*.
   Samsung: Settings → Safety and emergency → Medical info.
2. **Learn Emergency SOS.** iPhone: hold the side button + a volume button. Samsung: press the side key 5 times (turn it on in Safety and emergency). Try it once with the auto-call off.
3. **Text, don't call.** In a disaster, SMS often gets through when calls fail. Keep messages short.
4. **Pick one out-of-town contact.** Everyone in your family checks in with the same person outside Bangkok. Local lines jam first.
5. **Paper backup.** Write 5 key numbers on a card in your wallet and go-bag. Write your number on kids' arms with a marker on outings and at big events.
6. **Save battery:** low-power mode, dark mode, brightness down, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi off. In places with no signal, use airplane mode: hunting for signal drains the battery fast.
7. **Download offline maps** of Bangkok and your bug-out route (Google Maps → Offline maps). This app's map works offline too.
8. **A car is a charger.** A USB socket or 12 V adapter charges phones while the engine runs. Run it outdoors only.
9. **Photos of documents.** ID card, passport, house registration, insurance, prescriptions. Keep them in your phone (offline) and in cloud storage.
10. **3 of anything = help.** 3 whistle blasts, 3 flashes, 3 shouts. Repeat every minute.

## Water

1. **Freeze bottles now.** Fill clean bottles about 80% (ice expands) and keep them in the freezer. In a blackout they keep the freezer cold, then you drink them as they melt.
2. **Store water in bottles that held drinking water.** Never use milk or juice jugs: you can't get them clean. Write the date on each one and replace it every **6 months**.
3. **Hidden water at home:** ice cubes, the water heater tank, the liquid in canned fruit and veg, and the toilet **tank** (not the bowl, and not if it has blue cleaner). Boil or treat tank water before drinking.
4. **Budget 4 L per person per day** in Thai heat. A family of 3 for 3 days = 36 L, which is about 24 big 1.5 L bottles.

## Food

1. **No-cook noodles.** Instant noodles go soft in room-temperature safe water in about 15–20 minutes. Add the seasoning and eat.
2. **Soak rice first.** 30+ minutes of soaking cuts cooking time and fuel a lot.
3. **Retained-heat cooking (hay-box).** Boil the pot for 5 minutes, put the lid on, then wrap the whole pot in towels or blankets inside a cooler or box. Rice, beans and stews keep cooking with no more fuel. Eat it within a few hours.
4. **Eat the fridge first, then the freezer, then the cans.**
5. **2-hour rule:** meat, dairy, cooked rice and leftovers that sat above 4 °C for more than 2 hours go in the bin.
6. **Bad cans:** bulging, leaking, spurting or deeply dented on the seam? Throw it away without tasting.
7. **Pick foods you actually eat.** Canned tuna, sardines, crackers, peanut butter, nuts, dried fruit, energy bars. Rotate them into normal meals.

> **Warning:** When in doubt, throw it out. Food poisoning plus no clean water plus heat is a fast route to hospital.

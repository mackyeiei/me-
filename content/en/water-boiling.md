Boiling is the most reliable way to kill germs (bacteria, viruses, parasites) when you are not sure your water is safe.

## Steps

1. **If the water is cloudy:** let it settle, then pour the clear part through a clean cloth, coffee filter or paper towel.
2. Bring the water to a **rolling boil** (big bubbles that do not stop when you stir).
3. Keep it boiling for **1 minute**.
4. Above **2,000 m** altitude (e.g. top of Doi Inthanon is ~2,565 m), boil for **3 minutes**.
5. Let it cool **with a lid on**. Do not add ice unless the ice is from safe water.
6. Store in a **clean, covered** container. Pour out, do not dip cups in.

## Make it taste better

1. Pour it back and forth between two clean containers a few times.
2. Let it stand a few hours, or add a pinch of salt per litre.

## Limits

> **Warning:** Boiling does **not** remove chemicals, fuel, heavy metals or salt. If flood water smells of fuel or chemicals, **do not drink it** even after boiling. Find another source.

1. Bottled water is the safest choice if you have it.
2. No fuel? Use chlorine or SODIS (see the Water section).
3. Boiling uses a lot of fuel. Boil only what you need to drink and brush teeth.

## How much do you need?

1. Plan **4 L per person per day** in hot weather (drinking + basic hygiene).
2. Do not ration drinking water in heat. Drink what you need today and look for more tomorrow.

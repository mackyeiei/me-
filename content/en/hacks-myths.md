## Myth → what to do instead

1. **"Suck out snake venom, cut the bite, or put on a tight tourniquet."** ✗ → Keep the person **still and calm**, splint the limb, take off rings and watches, and get to hospital fast (call 1669). Thai hospitals stock antivenom. A photo of the snake helps, but don't try to catch it.
2. **"Pee on a jellyfish sting."** ✗ → Get out of the water and pour **vinegar** on it (many Thai beaches have vinegar posts for box jellyfish). Rinse with **seawater**, not fresh water. Severe pain or trouble breathing = 1669.
3. **"Drink seawater or urine if you run out."** ✗ → Both make dehydration worse. Save sweat instead: shade, rest, move at night.
4. **"Put butter, toothpaste, fish sauce or ice on a burn."** ✗ → Cool, running tap water for **20 minutes**, then cover loosely with cling film or a clean plastic bag.
5. **"Tilt your head back for a nosebleed."** ✗ → **Lean forward** and pinch the soft part of the nose for 10–15 minutes.
6. **"Put something in the mouth of someone having a seizure."** ✗ → Clear the space, cushion the head, time it. After it stops, roll them onto their side. Call 1669 if it lasts over 5 minutes.
7. **"Pull the knife or stick out of the wound."** ✗ → **Leave it in.** Press around it and get help. Pulling it out can make the bleeding much worse.
8. **"Shallow flood water is fine to walk or drive through."** ✗ → **15 cm** of moving water can knock you over and **30 cm** can float a car. In Bangkok, flood water also hides open drains, manholes and live wires.
9. **"The lift is the fastest way out of a fire."** ✗ → **Stairs only.** Lifts fail and open onto smoke-filled floors.
10. **"Just open the door to see where the fire is."** ✗ → Feel the door and handle with the **back of your hand** first. Hot = keep it shut and use another way out.
11. **"Run outside or stand in a doorway during an earthquake."** ✗ → **Drop, Cover, Hold On** under a sturdy table, away from windows. Go out only once the shaking stops.
12. **"Moss grows on the north side of trees."** ✗ → It is unreliable. Use the sun, a shadow stick or the stars (see Navigation).

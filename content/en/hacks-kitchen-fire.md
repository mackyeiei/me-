## Fires in the kitchen

1. **Grease fire: heat OFF, lid ON.** Slide a lid or a sheet tray over the pan and leave it there until it is completely cool.
2. **Small grease fire, no lid?** Dump on **baking soda or salt**. Never water: it explodes the oil. Never flour: it can burn.
3. **Never carry a burning pan** outside or to the sink.
4. **Oven or microwave fire:** turn it off and keep the door **closed**. The fire runs out of air.
5. **Extinguisher = P.A.S.S.** **P**ull the pin, **A**im at the base, **S**queeze, **S**weep side to side. If it is not out in about 10 seconds, leave and close the door behind you.
6. **Smell gas?** Don't touch light switches or lighters. Shut the tank valve, open windows, get out, and call from outside.

## Making fire when you need it

1. **Cotton balls + petroleum jelly (Vaseline)** = the best cheap fire starter. Each one burns for a couple of minutes. Pack 20 in a pill bottle or zip bag.
2. **Corn chips burn.** Oily snacks like Doritos light easily and burn for a while. Emergency tinder from 7-Eleven.
3. **Hand sanitizer gel** (60%+ alcohol) lights instantly. The flame is almost invisible in daylight, so be careful.
4. **Keep a lighter and matches in separate zip bags.** A wet lighter dries; wet matches don't.
5. **Charcoal stove (เตาถ่าน) outdoors only.** Indoors it makes carbon monoxide. Headache, dizziness and sleepiness are the warning signs. Get to fresh air.

> **Warning:** Never cook on gas, charcoal or a camp stove in a closed room, bathroom or tent. Carbon monoxide kills whole families in their sleep.

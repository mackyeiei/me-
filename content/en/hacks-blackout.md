1. **Headlamp + water bottle = room lantern.** Strap a headlamp around a full clear bottle with the light pointing in. The water spreads the light around the room.
2. **Leave one light switched ON.** When it comes on, the power is back. Switch everything else off.
3. **Unplug TV, computer, router and AC.** The power can surge when it comes back and kill electronics.
4. **Fill the bathtub and buckets as soon as the power goes.** Condo water pumps need electricity, so high floors lose water within hours. Use it for flushing: pour a bucket straight into the toilet bowl.
5. **Keep the fridge shut.** A closed fridge stays cold for about **4 hours**. A full freezer lasts about **48 hours**, a half-full one about 24.
6. **The coin trick.** Keep a cup of frozen water in the freezer with a coin on top. If you come back and the coin has sunk to the bottom, the freezer thawed. Throw out the meat.
7. **Stairs only.** Never take the lift during a blackout or when power is flickering. Bring the headlamp. Stairwells are pitch black.
8. **No candles.** LED lights only. Candles in a hot, crowded room are how blackouts turn into fires.
9. **Never run a generator, charcoal stove or gas grill indoors** or on an enclosed balcony. Carbon monoxide has no smell and kills in your sleep.
10. **Phone on low-power mode, brightness down.** Text instead of calling: texts get through when networks are jammed.
11. **Cool down before it gets dangerous.** Close curtains on the sunny side, wet a towel for your neck, sleep on the floor (cooler). See *Heat & haze hacks*.
12. **Check your neighbours.** Knock on the doors of older people and anyone living alone, especially on high floors.

> **Warning:** Downed power lines and flooded rooms with live sockets kill. Stay away from water near sockets until the main breaker is OFF.

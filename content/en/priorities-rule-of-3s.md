## The rule of 3s

A rough guide to what kills you first. Fix things in this order.

1. **3 minutes** without air or with heavy bleeding.
2. **3 hours** without shelter in harsh weather. In Thailand this mostly means **heat**: no shade + no cooling can turn into heat stroke in hours.
3. **3 days** without water. Less in heat or with hard work.
4. **3 weeks** without food.

> **Warning:** These are rough numbers, not targets. Children, older people and sick people have much less margin.

## First 5 minutes: S.T.O.P.

1. **Stop.** Do not run or rush. Panic wastes energy and causes injuries.
2. **Think.** What is the danger right now? Fire, water, falling objects, traffic, crowd?
3. **Observe.** Who is hurt? What do you have? Where is safe?
4. **Plan.** Pick one next action. Then do it.

## Order of priorities

1. **Get away from immediate danger** (fire, smoke, rising water, unstable building).
2. **Stop heavy bleeding, check breathing.** Call **1669** if anyone is badly hurt.
3. **Protect from heat or cold.** Shade, airflow, wet cloth in heat. Dry layers in cold and rain.
4. **Water.** Find it, make it safe, ration sweat, not water.
5. **Signal / communicate.** Tell someone where you are. Save phone battery.
6. **Food.** Last. You can go a long time without it.

## Keep your head

1. Do one task at a time.
2. Drink, eat a little and rest when you can. Tired people make bad calls.
3. Stay with your group unless your plan says otherwise.
4. Follow your **Plan** tab: trigger rules and rally points decided in advance beat guessing under stress.
